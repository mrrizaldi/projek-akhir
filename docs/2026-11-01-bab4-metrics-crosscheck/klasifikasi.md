# Crosscheck of classification metrics and measurement methods (Chapter 4) against standards and literature

Research date: 1 Nov 2026. The full text was read for 16 sources. Three other sources are only abstracts or are cited through secondary sources, and their status is marked in Section 2.

**Important limit.** The text of the ANSI/AAMI EC57 standard (editions 1998/(R)2008 and 2012/(R)2020) and EC38 is **not accessible** because it is paywalled. The EC57 content in this report comes from three routes:
(a) the official reference implementation `bxb`/`sumstats` and the guide "Evaluating ECG Analyzers" by G. B. Moody at PhysioNet. The `bxb` page states that the program "must be used to obtain the beat-by-beat performance statistics cited in EC38 and EC57";
(b) the description of EC57 in de Chazal 2004 and Luz 2016;
(c) the description of EC57 in the systematic review Silva 2025.
All three routes write the edition as **EC57:1998** or **EC57:1998/(R)2008**. No source was found that details the changes in the 2012 edition. The key `aami_ec57_2012` in the book should be rechecked: which edition the claims cited to that key actually come from.

| 1 | Binary class Normal vs Arrhythmia (S+V+F), positive = Arrhythmia | EC57 does not define a combined "arrhythmia" class. The EC57 beat statistics are **QRS** (detection), **VEB** (V vs non-V) and **SVEB** (S vs non-S), each with Se and +P, plus FPR for VEB. See the `bxb` report columns "Q Se, Q +P, V Se, V +P, V FPR" (+ "S Se, S +P" with the -L/-S option) in PhysioNet `bxb.c`/`sumstats.c`, and de Chazal 2004 pp. 1201–1202 and Table V(a),(b). ### Standards and reference implementations

**moody_wag_eval**: G. B. Moody, "Evaluating ECG Analyzers" (WFDB Applications Guide, PhysioNet, updated 10 Jun 2022). https://physionet.org/physiotools/wag/eval.htm (subpages evnode8, evnode10). Status: TL (HTML). Quotes:
- "the evaluation protocol allows a time difference of up to 150 ms between each pair of matching beat annotations." (evnode8, Obtaining Test Annotation Files)
- "All beat annotations are mapped during the evaluation process into the set { N, V, F, S, Q } (corresponding to normal, ventricular ectopic, ventricular fusion, supraventricular ectopic, and unclassifiable or paced beats respectively)" (evnode8)
- "Any beat annotations that appear in the first five minutes of a record (the 'learning period') are ignored in the evaluation process." (evnode8)
- "bxb compares annotation files beat by beat; its output includes QRS, VEB, and (optionally) SVEB sensitivity and positive predictivity, as well as RR interval error and shutdown statistics." (evnode10)
- "Only the statistics required by EC38 and EC57 are reported by ecgeval." (evnode10)

**physionet_bxb**: G. B. Moody, BXB(1) "ANSI/AAMI-standard beat-by-beat annotation comparator", https://physionet.org/physiotools/wag/bxb-1.htm. Program sources `bxb.c` and `sumstats.c` at https://github.com/bemoody/wfdb (app/). Status: TL (HTML + source code). Quotes:
- "bxb is the reference implementation of these algorithms, and must be used to obtain the beat-by-beat performance statistics cited in EC38 and EC57 in order to be in compliance with these standards (see EC38, section 5.2.14, and EC57, section 4.2)."
- "-f time Begin the comparison at the specified time (default: 5 minutes after the beginning of the record)." "-w time Set the match window (default: 0.15 seconds)".
- "The -f, -O, -t, and -w options modify the comparison algorithm used by bxb in ways not permitted by EC38 or EC57."
- `bxb.c`: `VFN = Vn + Vs + Vf + Vq + Vo + Vx; VFP = Nv + Sv + Ov + Xv; SFN = Sn + Sv + Sf + Sq + So + Sx; SFP = Ns + Vs + Fs + Os + Xs;` (upper case = reference, lower case = test, o = missed, O = false detection).
- `bxb.c` (mapping comment): "beats which are either paced (type PACE) or fusions of paced and normal beats (type PFUS) are treated in the same way as unknown beats."
- `sumstats.c`: "derives the aggregate statistics described in sections 3.5.2 and 3.5.3 of ... ANSI/AAMI EC57:1998 ... and in sections 4.2.14.4.1 and 4.2.14.4.2 of ... EC38:1998". The output contains the "Gross" and "Average" rows.

**aami_ec57**: ANSI/AAMI EC57 (1998/(R)2008; 2012/(R)2020). Status: **not accessed** (paywalled). Its content is known only through the sources above and below.

## 3. Most important gaps (ordered from most risky at the defence)

1. **DS2 metrics are measured with oracle R positions, while the claim is about the device.** The EC57 beat statistics are end-to-end: `bxb` counts V beats missed by the detector as FN and falsely detected beats labelled V as FP. With a Pan-Tompkins Se of 0.9469, about 5% of beats never reach the classifier, and this is not reflected in the recall of 0.92 (V) or in the aggregate recall. Action: report two metric levels, namely classifier (annotation R) and system (Pan-Tompkins R, matching ≤150 ms, missed arrhythmia beat = FN). The alignment chain is already available in `eval_detected_segmentation.py`.
2. **Use of DS2 for ablation.** If even one final value (window, HOS, architecture) was chosen from DS2 figures, selection bias arises (Cawley & Talbot 2010; Kapoor & Narayanan 2023 L1.3). The literature does it too (Mondéjar Table 5, Saenz-Cogollo Fig. 3, Farag Table 4), but an examiner who knows inter-patient evaluation will ask about it. Action: show that the decision was taken on VAL/DS1, or label the DS2 ablation "post-hoc".
3. **QRS detection is reported with Se only, and the figure is low.** EC57 requires QRS Se **and** +P with a 150 ms window, plus a 5-minute learning period. Se 0.9469 is far from the "<0.5% error rate" quoted by de Chazal 2004 p. 1205. Action: report Se, +P, the matching window, and the treatment of the first 5 minutes. Explain the cause of the low Se with data (which records contribute the most FN).
4. **The 0.02 quantisation threshold rests on a reference that is not comparable.** Zambrano measures accuracy with a per-beat split (the authors themselves admit leakage), and there is no standard threshold. Busia shows that the INT8 impact is concentrated in the sensitivity of class S. Action: state 0.02 as a self-designed threshold. Report Δ per metric, Δrecall per class S/V/F, and the percentage of beats whose label changes.
5. **Metrics for imbalanced data and the EC57 statistics are incomplete.** ROC-AUC without PR-AUC at a prevalence of ≈11% is open to criticism (Saito 2015; Chicco & Jurman 2020/2023). The EC57 "average" statistic is also absent (only the median so far), and the 5-minute exclusion is not mentioned. Action: add PR-AUC with the prevalence line, MCC, the mean per record, and one sentence on the protocol (whole record, Q discarded at 7 beats, following the de Chazal/Mondéjar convention).
